## Brand & Style

This design system blends Swiss structural precision with empathetic humanitarian storytelling. It transforms institutional reporting into a collectible publication format, balancing journalistic authority with human warmth. 

The aesthetic is grounded in classical editorial layouts: generous margins, dramatic typographic scale shifts, asymmetric column pairings, and tactile print references. The emotional register is dignified, urgent, and transparent—avoiding sterile corporate dashboards while shunning patronizing non-profit tropes. Visual weight is anchored by crisp ink tones and elevated through warm terracotta and ochre accents reminiscent of sun-drenched earth and vitality.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the tactile quality of high-end newsprint and heavy art stock.

- **Primary (`#0A192F` - Deep Ink Navy):** The structural anchor. Used for primary narrative titles, dark cover spreads, formal borders, and high-impact structural blocks.
- **Secondary (`#E05A47` - Terracotta Coral):** The emotional focal point. Used for metric highlights, quotation marks, active chapter indicators, and urgent narrative calls.
- **Tertiary (`#F29C38` - Sunburst Ochre):** Secondary data tier and geographic markers. Balances terracotta in multi-series charts and badge accents.
- **Quaternary Forest Teal (`#1B4947`):** Represents environmental, sustainability, and fieldwork programs. Used in contextual story tags, secondary cards, and data comparison matrices.
- **Canvas Ivory (`#F9F8F5`):** The universal background color replacing pure digital white to reduce harshness and simulate unbleached matte paper.
- **Surface Muted Gray (`#F0EFEA`):** Applied to card backgrounds, callout panels, and table alternate stripes.
- **Body Neutral (`#1A1A1A` - Charcoal Ink):** Used for long-form editorial copy to provide high legibility without the jarring starkness of pure black.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dialogue between the literary gravitas of `Playfair Display` and the functional clarity of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Hierarchy:** Chapter headers and impact statements leverage `display-xl` and `headline-lg` with tightened letter spacing, creating the feel of a bespoke broadsheet or hardbound monograph.
- **Narrative Rhythm:** Article introductions employ `body-lead`, transitioning into multi-column `body-md` with generous line-height (`1.625`) to preserve comfortable reading over long texts.
- **Data & Micro-Layouts:** Numerical metrics utilize `stat-display` in tabular-lining figures when comparative accuracy is required. Metadata, folios, section numbering, and table headings must always be set in `label-caps` (uppercase with `0.12em` tracking).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a dual-mode 12-column Swiss grid that dynamically reconfigures between strict data displays and expressive asymmetric editorial spreads.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and `4rem` outer page margins. Narrative sections often offset text across 7 columns with an adjacent 4-column statistical callout or pull-quote, leaving 1 column of breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins. Sidebars collapse beneath main narratives or form side-by-side metric pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Metrics and editorial photo masks convert to a single-column stacked cadence.
- **Rhythm & Page Folio Rules:** Vertical rhythm follows a strict base unit of `0.75rem` (12px). Editorial sections are separated by `space-2xl` to mimic deliberate page turns in a printed publication. Running folios and chapter titles maintain an aligned baseline along the outer margin edges.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy drop shadows, synthetic blurs, and glassmorphic treatments in favor of editorial paper layers and surface-level ink interactions.

- **Surface Tiers:** Depth is established by layering `#F0EFEA` (Warm Light Gray) and `#0A192F` (Deep Navy) containers over the `#F9F8F5` (Canvas Ivory) field.
- **Hairline Rules:** Structural division relies on 1px crisp borders (`#0A192F` at 15% opacity or solid `#E05A47` for section dividers), mimicking print plate registration marks and grid dividing lines.
- **Flat Inset Elevation:** In digital interactive contexts (e.g., downloadable chapters or interactive maps), hover states shift surface tonality subtly or activate an offset hard outline (e.g., `2px solid #0A192F`) rather than casting atmospheric shadows.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is uncompromisingly architectural and sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Corners:** Standard buttons, cards, containers, and data cells have crisp 90-degree right angles (`0px` border radius), reflecting the clean trims of guillotine-cut paper.
- **Geometric Photo Masks:** Editorial photography employs specific architectural crop forms:
  - Pure rectilinear edge-to-edge crops.
  - Asymmetric arched crops (where only the top edge features an architectural semi-circle arch while the base remains sharp).
  - Diptych and triptych split frames separated by a 2px Ivory seam.
- **Exceptions:** Pill-shaped capsules are strictly reserved for inline numerical metric tags, country code badges, and category labels to contrast the surrounding sharp layout structure.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Editorial Button:** Solid `#0A192F` fill, pure white `Plus Jakarta Sans` text, uppercase `label-caps` styling, 0px border radius, with `16px 32px` padding. Hover state transitions background to `#E05A47` with sharp 150ms ease.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** 1.5px solid border in `#0A192F`, transparent fill, with trailing typographic arrow (`→`). Hover adds `#F0EFEA` background tint.

### Data Metric Callout Cards
- Composed of an ivory or `#F0EFEA` background enclosed by a 1px border.
- The top edge features an uppercase classification tag (`label-caps`) in `#1B4947`.
- The number is set in `stat-display` in `#0A192F` or `#E05A47`.
- Metric tags and comparative trends (e.g., "+34% YoY") are housed within full pill-shaped badges (`border-radius: 9999px`) in `#F29C38` or `#E05A47` at 15% opacity with dark text.

### Pull Quotes
- Oversized opening quotation marks set in `Playfair Display` at `96px` in `#E05A47`, hanging outside the left margin of the quote text.
- Text rendered in `headline-md` italic, accompanied by a 2px horizontal rule in `#0A192F` (width: 48px) separating quote from attribution.
- Attribution formatted in `label-caps` naming the community member, region, and project year.

### Running Page Folios & Section Divider Ribbons
- **Page Folio Header/Footer:** Placed at the margins of every view; left side displays publication title and current section, right side displays current report page index (`04 // 48`) in `label-caps`.
- **Divider Ribbon:** A multi-tone editorial divider featuring a thick horizontal line (3px) in `#0A192F` adjacent to a narrow terracotta accent bar (3px) in `#E05A47`, accompanied by bold chapter number typography (`01`, `02`, `03`).

### Editorial Tables & Charts
- **Tables:** No vertical borders. Header rows set in `#0A192F` background with white `label-caps` text. Data rows alternate between transparent and `#F0EFEA` fills, bound by 1px bottom borders. All numerical data columns are right-aligned.
- **Infographics & Charts:** Primary bars/series use `#0A192F`, `#E05A47`, `#F29C38`, and `#1B4947`. Gridlines use dashed 1px stroke at 10% opacity `#0A192F`. Zero-baselines are rendered solid 1.5px `#0A192F`.

### Form Fields & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Minimalist baseline-only underline (1.5px solid `#0A192F`) on `#F0EFEA` background, transitioning to `#E05A47` underline on active focus.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square geometries with 1.5px stroke, checked state fills solid `#0A192F` with sharp contrast white checkmarks.
